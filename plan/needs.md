# Needs

The den controller does not exist as a product. `console/server.py` is a loopback sketch. It is not the start, and it is not installed.

## What the seats did

`develop` has no section implementation. The plan work is on `section/niches`, eleven commits, not merged here until this note says so.

Twenty-three `r*` branches each have one commit past the design base `18ab6fb`. They are drafts. They are not reviewed and they are not on `develop`. The later section seats, the ones cut as `section/<name>` and `section/<name>-gN`, did not move their branches past `develop`. Four doc branches were cut and added no unique commit. Treat that wave as not done.

## Requirements

2. Before any project, onboarding reports present or missing for GitHub, Hetzner, OmniRoute, Cloudflare, Tailscale, and Headscale. Notion and Obsidian stay missing until a path is named. No secret in the packet. Plan: `plan/start.md`.
3. A section seat that is unsure gets one explore or deep-dive crutch. The crutch does not decide.
4. OmniRoute provisions stay opt-in. `plan/omniroute.md`.
5. Safety limits are copied into the seat prompt and into niche notes. They are not dropped.
6. Draft `r*` commits stay on their branches until a check seat reads them and a second note agrees. Then one section branch is pushed for comparison. They do not land in a batch.

## Space for the controller

Nothing in `cmd/` or `web/` is reserved yet, because those directories are the build. The space is the packet in `plan/controller.md`: API seat, page seat, probe seat, check seat. Schedules are an empty list until a file exists. Start and Stop are not in the first build.

The sketch may keep port 8792 until that binary exists. The binary does not share the port with the sketch.

## Research conclusion

The harness does not need a new framework to see its own work. It needs `develop` to carry this plan, the drafts to stay reviewable, and the controller left unbuilt until a human starts that packet.

Fox, foxd, and floki stay the company product. radar stays the private price and tracker list. den is the harness. The controller is a window onto den, not a second brain.
