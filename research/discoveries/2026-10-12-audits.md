- `run_argv` in `sections/security/match.py` refuses `| & ; < >` and a newline. A direct call still accepts `$(…)`, backticks, a glob, and a NUL byte. No executor in the tree runs that argv yet.
- `path_jail` is only in `sections/security/match.py` and its test. No Go file calls it. `denread` has a separate jail.
- `internal/fetch/page.go` `Public()` refuses private hosts and is tested. `plan/BASE.md` and `plan/foundation.md` do not name it.
- `sections/loop/LEAD.md` says three controls. `plan/foundation.md` lists five. Two of the five have no code.
- `plan/BASE.md` and `plan/foundation.md` do not list the same fields a child packet keeps.
- Load-bearing words without one definition: section root and packet root, host we own, human side record, the vault and the repo.
- No refusal ceiling, no cap on Read size, argv length, or Write size, and no fallback when the jail root is invalid.
